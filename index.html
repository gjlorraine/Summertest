estival

of, characteristic of, or occurring in summer

summer solstice

estival rhythms

Expansion, Connection, Growth, Creativity, Vitality, Manifestation, Community, Fruition

pentecost

The season of the Christian calendar following Easter focused on the outpouring of the Holy Spirit, the birth of the church, and the commissioning of Jesus's followers to join God's enlivening mission for life over death. The theological themes of this season include regeneration, growth in grace, reconciliation in community, spiritual gifts for discipleship, and active hope for the new creation.
